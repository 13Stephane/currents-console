Reliability

Predicted confidence vs empirical frequency, Wilson 95% bars, point area ∝ n. Raw model values are internal (mono-dim); empirical values in gold. Region below the diagonal is overconfidence.

Floor

Three floors on one strip. Divergence is the signal: current ≪ actuarial leaks weak surfacings; current ≫ revealed starves the loop against the user's own bar. Nothing here is applied — the proposal is copied and applied through the normal config path.
