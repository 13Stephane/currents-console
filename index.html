News

The news-fit gate — the precision filter that runs before the resonator turns a story into theses, so nothing here writes a calibration signal (model proposes, you dispose). Eligible = flowing (the default). Parked = low-fit set-aside: the system parks these automatically, they expire from view after 14 days and train the scorer (also where Park mutes one yourself). Held = your manual shelf: never auto-set, never expires, kept to revisit. Validate promotes a parked/held story back into flow. Per item you can turn it into a thesis (a landscape sounding for your Theses feed) or analyze what it already touches. — vs the Theses feed, where disposing does write calibration: that's the ground truth, this is the gate.
